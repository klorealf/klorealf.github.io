About me

I'm Khadija Franklin. I started in the lab, in quality control and then as a chemist at Perrigo, before moving to Coptis, where I've spent the last seven years on the software side of the cosmetics industry.

As a Customer Success and Key Account Manager, I look after a portfolio of 71 US accounts and have led technical implementation for every new client since 2019. My chemistry training means I speak the same language as the formulators and regulatory teams I work with.

I'm now combining that background with software and AI to build practical tools for cosmetic science: ingredient checks, regulatory lookups, and the everyday workflows that slow labs down.
